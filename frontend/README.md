NilePixel Tech — Landing Page
🌟 Overview
NilePixel Tech is a group of intuitive developers that blends heritage and innovation.
Inspired by the Nile — a symbol of life, connection, and growth — and combined with the Pixel, the building block of modern digital creation, NilePixel Tech represents a team rooted in African identity yet reaching global standards.

Our motto — “Design. Develop. Deliver.” — captures the full creative cycle: crafting beautiful ideas, building solid solutions, and delivering high-quality results. This landing page reflects our commitment to excellence in design, web development, and tutoring.

Fully responsive React.js + Bootstrap 5 design
Smooth navigation with React Router (Home, Signup, Login)
Elegant Hero section with call-to-action
About section highlighting our philosophy and values
Services section showcasing "Design, Develop, Deliver"
"Get Started" button linking to Signup page and "Get in Touch" linking to Login page
